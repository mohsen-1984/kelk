## 1. What changed

[`docs/HELDOUT.md`](../HELDOUT.md) is the protocol: which sets are held out, who may open what, how an opening is
recorded, how the sets rotate, what every report checks.

| Guard | What it is | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Fuzz held-out | 100 style-fuzzed PDFs (`make-fuzz.js heldout`); `run-fuzz.js heldout` keeps per-file results in memory — `_scores.json` holds the summary alone, no Markdown; `--show` names files and logs the opening; seeds rotate every 6 months (next 1406-02-12) or after an opening, the base bumped by 100000 | mean, ltr / rtl, `rtl` column, TOL 1 |
| Real-producer held-out | every lab PDF of a held-out document, in any folder: the split is **by document name** (the part after `--`), the held-out documents a committed list `HELD_DOCS` that adding documents never shrinks; a new document held out by its name's hash (< 0.30); an opened document moves to dev and only new documents refill | `run-import.js --real-heldout`: one mean (total, `rtl` column), the count and the sorted file names; TOL 1; a missing result fails; `--update` refuses a stale result |
| Held-out goldens | the five P4.2 goldens on held-out PDFs: `golden.js` writes their mean only | TOL 1 |
| Private real files | a user's own PDFs, never committed: `snapshot.js` before / after, `--against` | none (reference-free) |
| Opened log | `docs/reports/heldout-opened.log`, **committed** with the change that opened something; written by `--show`, `--show-held` and the `openHeld()` guard in `env.js` (`dbg.js`, `dump.js`, `cmp.js`, `lines.js`, `shapes.js`, `words.js`, `snapshot.js`); `run-crash.js` reads held-out files without naming them; the gate names the log whenever it has lines | note |

Held-out documents after P4.3: **`real-deck-fa`, `real-letter-ar`, `real-memo-fa`, `real-report-fa`** (17 PDFs); dev:
9 documents (27 PDFs). The opened log is empty.

**Before the split existed** the four documents' PDFs were scored per file once (P4.1's run and its join). That is
recorded in HELDOUT.md: no rule may be tuned on what was seen then; the first rotation of the real side replaces them
with new documents.
