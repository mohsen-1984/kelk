### Decisions

1. **Controls for the generator side**: a Chromium variant is compared with a control drawn in the same run by the
   same recipe, never with the shipped seed — otherwise the local Chromium and fonts would be measured too.
2. **`renderMode` copies text only** (a full copy painted its fills over the page's own text: another drawing).
3. **`hfInject` at the paper's edges** (at 22 pt it ran into one line with Chrome's printed header).
4. **`rotate90` / `rotate180` rebuilt** (review): turned content + `/Rotate`, the case the importer must handle.
   The pure-`/Rotate` `v1` variants were dropped: on upright content they pass by construction and test nothing.
5. **`stamp` relabelled "hard"** (review): the plan's "near", but it loses 32.7 and breaks block ± 1 on 23 of 24
   files; a "hard" transform is measured and gated at 3 points.
6. **Allow-list per file with a floor** (review): a listed file may not get worse unseen (1-point tolerance) nor
   crash; a new mismatch on another seed of the same transform fails.
7. **Relations are measured, not gated** beyond a note when a count rises; the byte-identical assertion is the gated
   rule.
8. **The gate compares per transform** (version, env, common seeds). The committed `baseline.json` meta
   (generator 2) has none of these records: **until the lead rebuilds the baseline on CI (§5), every meta transform
   is a note in baseline mode**; CI's `--against base` (both importers on the same PDFs) compares all of them
   meanwhile.
9. **qpdf pinned to noble's release build** `11.9.0-1.1build1` (determined from packages.ubuntu.com: release
   `11.9.0-1.1build1`, noble-updates `11.9.0-1.1ubuntu0.1`; both print «qpdf version 11.9.0»). The release pocket
   never changes, so the pin cannot disappear; the package version is also recorded in the env (`qpdfPkg`) so a
   change of build is not compared blindly. The pin is not yet exercised on a runner.
10. `color` makes nothing for `word-memo--memo-fa` and `itext-word--circular-fa` (no dark colour operator: they draw
    in the default black) — expected `∅` notes, 22 files.

## 6. Open issues

- **`/Rotate` is not applied by the importer** (rotate90 −82.4, rotate180 −83.0): every landscape Word page and every
  upside-down scan reads as turned text. The largest item of P3.3's order.
- **Text outside the CropBox is kept** on one-page documents (cropbox: slug lines read as text).
- The other 12 known exceptions (§2) and the large losses — `stamp` −32.7 (diagonal watermark letters read into the
  text), the Tc/Tz family, `noToUnicode` — are P3.3's work list.
- The generator side covers 5 seeds; Word / LibreOffice / Kelk generator-side variants need their own recipes, and
  `make-meta.js` copies make-chrome.js / make-rtl.js's CSS by hand (a drift moves control and variants together).
- `renderMode` skips a page whose text lies only in forms (none of the 24 seeds is all forms).
- The qpdf pin is untested on a runner.
