## 1. What changed

Before P3.4 the importer measured degradation (`r.quality` flags) but never acted on it, and a page whose extraction
or layout threw was lost (`pageFailed`). `lib/pdfimport/ladder.js` adds a confidence per page and a ladder of rungs.

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| Page confidence | 0–1 from lost and guessed glyphs, glued words, text under a cropped picture, the structure tree's disagreement, ambiguous gaps and too few gap samples (registered thresholds, `_t`) |
| `full` → `simplified` | low confidence, or suspect spaces with lost glyphs, or a layout that failed: headings and paragraphs only (tables, lists, quotes off) |
| → `textContent` | an extraction that failed, or lost glyphs where pdf.js's own text is demonstrably better (at most half the lost share, 80 % of the characters kept): the page from pdf.js's `getTextContent()` |
| → `ocr` | a page with no text at all under a covering picture: a note and the page image (the OCR engine is P7) |
| `mode` | `auto` (the ladder), `safe` (every page simplified — the UI's "Re-import in safe mode"), `full` (no ladder; a failed page reported as `failed`) |
| Result | `r.pages[i] = { page, mode, conf, why, failed? }` and `r.report` (the contract P3.5's panel reads) |
| Lab | three PDFs that must demote (`tests/pdf-lab/ladder/`, `make-ladder.js`): a scan (→ ocr), a glued Type 3 file and one with 25 % lost glyphs (→ simplified); checked by `run-stress.js`; `run-crash.js` sweeps them (also in CI) |

**The textContent rung reads right-to-left text in logical order.** pdf.js gathers an item's characters in drawing
order and runs its own bidi on each item, without mirroring; many items are single glyphs. The rung undoes that
per-item reordering (a port of pdf.js's reorder, `tcVisual`), spreads each item over its width as glyphs, and lets
the importer's own bidi read the line (`_joinLines` → `_logical`, `repairArabic`): numbers, dates, percent signs and
brackets come out as written. ZWNJ is restored from Chrome's evidence (measured against the importer's glyphs on the
RTL corpus: 99 / 99 and 27 / 27); list numbers open paragraphs; a short line larger than the body becomes a heading;
running heads, feet and lone page numbers repeated on neighbour pages are dropped.
