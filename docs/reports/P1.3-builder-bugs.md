## 3. Decisions

1. **B3 split, not clamp:** splitting keeps every word and left every existing PDF unchanged; clamping would cut
   content. Cells of a split group are top-aligned; a header cell spanning into body rows is not repeated; header
   rows taller than half a page are not repeated.
2. A spanning cell takes the fill of its first row.
3. **Letters without presentation forms** keep their nominal glyph and their neighbours join them. Treating them as
   non-joining would break the round trip: the importer reads a dual-joining letter that does not join forward as a
   lost ZWNJ.
4. Joining types from `ArabicShaping.txt` as a table of their own (Arabic and Arabic Supplement; Extended-A/B not).
5. The colour table holds all CSS names: the PDF builder used to draw `purple`, `teal` and `rgb()` colours black.
6. `HtmlBuilder.save()` now returns the builder, like the others.
7. The `fa-features` zero had two causes and both were fixed: the scorer (a correct import could not score) and four
   left-to-right assumptions in the quote rules. The scorer change was checked to move no score of the unchanged
   importer.
8. The `RTL_LETTER_PATTERN` gap found by the shaping cases is fixed here (one line in `BidiCore.js`) rather than
   left as a row: the cases needed it, and the lab shows no corpus file moved by it.
9. The lab ran as four parallel background processes on one machine (corpus, fuzz dev, fuzz held-out, meta), then
   the gate twice; CI's `pdf-lab` run on the PR gives the official base-against-head figures (§5).

## 6. Open issues

- Eight Arabic letters (ؠ ػ ؼ ؾ ؿ ۺ ۻ ۼ) have no glyph in any bundled PDF font (`lib/pdf-base64-ttf-fonts/`) —
  a fallback font when someone needs them.
- `tools/bidi-lab.html` keeps its own pasted copy of BidiCore (old colour table, old letter range) — P8.2 makes it
  load `lib/BidiCore.js`.
- LibreOffice and wkhtmltopdf `fa-features` stay at quote 0 for their own reasons (broken text and lists in those
  PDFs), not this rule.
- A single line taller than a page inside a table cell still overflows (warning only), as designed.
- `table-tall-misaligned` round trip 90.9 % / 90.3 % (grey): the importer drops the word "plain" from some lines
  beside the 60 small images and takes ≈ 8 s on them — an importer matter for P3.
- A transparent *border* colour still becomes black in DOCX (`DocxBuilder.js:1759`) and passes through in DOC
  (`WordHtmlBuilder.js:1696`): it needs a border-style change, not only the colour.
- `softJoin` keeps the scorer's blindness to block boundaries above a joined quote line (review finding 7); the
  KNOWN entry for `shaping-no-forms-uncovered` would hide a regression in that case.
