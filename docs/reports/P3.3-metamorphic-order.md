## 1. What changed

Six batches in parallel, one per cause, in the metamorphic net's order of losses; each measured with the whole lab
(corpus, fuzz dev, fuzz held-out as means, the 20-transform meta net with qpdf, the gate) and kept only where it
paid. An independent review tried each new rule on PDFs built to make it misfire; its findings were applied.

| Batch | Cause found | Change | Net |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/Rotate` | the importer read user space and ignored a page's `/Rotate` | `extract.js _pageFrame`: the upright view from pdf.js's viewport (y up, at the page's origin); the operator walk starts in it, so glyphs, paths, clips, images, links and the page box come out upright; figure crops and OCR ink through `_pageViewport` | `rotate90` 0/24 → **24/24**, `rotate180` 0/24 → **24/24** (−82.4 / −83.0 → 0.0); 48 `KNOWN` entries removed |
| Word spaces under tracking | runs placed from untracked widths drift by the tracking; justified lines that draw their spaces read as columns | `calibration.js untrack` + drift regression, `lines.js _glyphRows` re-lays out drifted runs; `justGap` in `_segments` | `tz85` −9.2 → **−0.1**, `trackTight` −3.6 → **0.0**, `trackLoose` −12.2 → **−0.8** (plan: each ≥ −2); fuzz dev 86.6 → 86.8 |
| Watermark | a diagonal grey stamp read into the text | `extract.js _watermark`: a run turned off the axes, ≥ 1.5 × the body, a neutral grey at low opacity or light, over no dark fill or picture, spanning 40 % of the page, on a page that keeps upright body text — dropped and noted (`watermarkDropped`, its words) | `stamp` 0/24 → **24/24** (−32.7 → 0.0) |
| `/ActualText` in object streams | the page dictionary looked up only among plain objects | `_pageMarkedTexts` falls back to `_rawObjStms()` | `objstm` 22/24 → **24/24** — the plan's "objstm ActualText read" |
| Scaled pages | a margin mirrored from one line past the page edge; sizes rounded to half points | margins mirrored only from a side ≥ 2 lines show; the bold-at-body test on drawn sizes (`_metrics.bodyExact`) | `scale80` 18/24 → **20/24** (−1.4 → −0.2) |
| Fonts with no ToUnicode | codes read as their own numbers; the space glyph lost; no program chosen among several of one name | `_gsubByCode`, `_gsubByWidth`, `_gsubNamed`, `_gsubByCodeGlyph`: every glyph named from its font program; whether codes are glyph ids decided per font from its CIDFont dictionary (`_cidGlyphIds`, packed or not) | `noToUnicode` −81.0 → **−46.1**, 11 of 24 seeds exact (the rest hold no Unicode at all: Chrome Type 3, LibreOffice / Prince / wkhtmltopdf subsets) |

Also: `pagesLinks` in `_toHtml` removed (always empty — the links come from `_allLinks`; the owner's Part C item 5).

### Measured and rejected (the plan's literal migrations)

The plan's items 2–4 (leading and paragraph-gap literals → `cal.steps`, heading sizes → `cal.body.size`, absolute pt
→ em) were each run through the whole lab and **did not pay**: every text-relative pt entry × body broke one corpus
file (a link lost to a trailing comma) and moved nothing in the scaled nets; leading from `cal.steps` joined
paragraphs on 6 corpus files (in one-line-paragraph documents the commonest baseline step is the paragraph pitch); nine
heading-size steps × body fell below the half-point rounding of line sizes. A calibrated `wordGapBareEm` cost dev
−0.2. The scaled-net losses had other causes (above). So **no registry entry is switched to calibrated** after P3.3:
the registry and the calibration are in place, and every gain came from finding the cause. The plan's "≥ 200 of ≈ 400
literals registered" is met (1,007, P3.2). **For the owner:** whether P3.3's scope stands as done this way.
