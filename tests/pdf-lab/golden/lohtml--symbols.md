# Symbols, Emoji and Extended Characters

This page collects characters that often break text extraction. Each line should come back exactly as written.

## Emoji

Single emoji: 😀 🚀 🎉 📄 🔒 ⏰ 🌍 🍕

Text-default symbols shown as emoji: ✔️ ❤️ ⚠️ ✏️ ☎️ ♻️

Skin tones and people: 👍🏽 👋🏿 👩‍💻 👨‍👩‍👧 🧑‍🔬

Flags and keycaps: 🇮🇷 🇯🇵 🇧🇷 #️⃣ 1️⃣ 🔟

Currency and units: € 1,250 · £ 980 · ¥ 12,000 · ₹ 5,400 · ₿ 0.25 · 25 °C · 5 µm · 90 % · ½ ¼ ¾

## Ligatures and typography

Words that use ligatures: office, affinity, efficient, fjord, The theory, waffle, scaffolding.

Quotes and dashes: “double” ‘single’ «guillemets» — em dash – en dash … ellipsis · middle dot • bullet

Non-breaking space between 10 kg and a thin space before units: 5 km
