# Community Garden Plan

Proposal to the residents' association, November 2026

## Why a garden

- The empty lot behind block C has been unused for six years
- Thirty households asked for space to grow vegetables
- Schools nearby want a place for outdoor lessons

## What we propose

- Twenty-four raised beds, each rented for a season
- A tool shed and a rain-water tank
- Two benches and a shaded corner for classes

## Costs

| Item | Cost (EUR) | Paid by |
|---|---|---|
| Beds and soil | 3,600 | Bed rents |
| Shed and tank | 2,100 | City grant |
| Benches and shade | 900 | Donations |

## Next step

Vote on the plan at the December meeting
