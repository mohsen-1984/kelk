# Tasks for the club party

## Numbers with a bracket, no space

1. Prepare the main hall of the library
2. Hire chairs and tables
3. Send an invitation to every member
4. Take a group photo at the end of the party with every guest, the new members and the staff of the library, and send it to everyone

## Numbers with a bracket, with and without a space

1. Tea and cakes
2. Books as gifts
3. Flowers for the speaker
4. Balloons for the children

## Numbers with a period

1. Read the report of the year
2. Welcome the new members
3. Hand out the gifts

## Bullets

- A table for signing up
- A table for selling books
- Some chairs for children
- A corner for photos

## Tasks and their parts

1. Prepare the hall
   - Set out the chairs
   - Turn on the lights
2. Refreshments
   - Make the tea
   - Lay out the cakes

Every member can take one of these tasks.
