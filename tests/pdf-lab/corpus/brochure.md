# Call for Partners in Digital Services

Third edition

Prepared by: Office of Digital Projects

## Introduction

The relief society is moving its services online, from volunteer sign-up to emergency requests. This call invites universities, companies and research centres to propose joint projects, and describes what the society can offer them in return: data, pilot sites and a nationwide network of branches.

Requests by channel, 2025

The chart shows how requests reached the society last year. Phone calls still carry most of the load, but requests through the mobile app have doubled in a year, and most new volunteers now sign up online.

## How to take part

Send a two-page proposal with the problem it addresses, the team and a first budget. Proposals are reviewed every month; the selected teams are invited to present their work at the society's headquarters.
