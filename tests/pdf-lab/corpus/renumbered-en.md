# The reading club

The reading club of the neighbourhood met every week last year, and its members grew from forty to more than a hundred and twenty. Most of them are school pupils and students, and the meetings are held in the small hall of the library.

At every meeting one book is introduced and the members talk about it. The members choose the books of each season by a vote, and the library provides enough copies of every book.

Next year the club plans two writing workshops, a party at the end of the season and a visit to the printing house of the town. The cost is covered by donations and by the membership fee.

New members can sign up at the first meeting of every month. A library card is all they need, and the meetings are free for everyone.
