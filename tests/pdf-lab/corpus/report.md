# Quarterly Operations Report

Prepared by the Operations Office for the executive committee. All figures are in thousands of US dollars unless stated otherwise.

## Summary

Revenue grew in every region during the third quarter, led by the Asia-Pacific office. Operating costs rose more slowly than revenue, so the operating margin improved by two points. Headcount was flat.
