# Smart Interview Platform

Product overview for partners, October 2026

## Introducing the interview system

The platform interviews job applicants online, by voice and video, in real time. Applicants first upload their résumé; the system reads it, compares it with the open positions and suggests the ones that fit their skills. Once an applicant picks a position, the interview starts: the questions are written for that position, the answers are spoken, and every answer is scored against the same criteria.

## Features

Interview rooms at the pilot site

- Résumé analysis against each position's competency model
- Spoken interviews with follow-up questions
- A report for the recruiter after every interview
- A chatbot that helps applicants improve their résumé
