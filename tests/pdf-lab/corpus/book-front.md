# About the Author

**Mina Darvish** is a data architect and teacher who has spent fifteen years building reporting systems for public libraries and small museums. She writes a monthly column on open data, mentors new analysts through a volunteer network, and speaks at regional conferences on catalog design. She currently leads the archive services team at a city library, where her work brings digital collections and reading rooms closer together. She is the author of two practical guides on metadata and lives near the coast, where she volunteers at a small maritime museum and catalogs its collection of ship logs, letters and harbour maps.

# About the Technical Reviewer

**Omid Rahnama** is a software engineer and a long-time reviewer of technical books. Over a career spanning research labs, a ferry company and two weather services, he has built scheduling tools, booking systems and data pipelines for timetables, tide tables and forecasts that run quietly in the background every hour of the day. He now leads a small platform group, where he looks after the tools other teams build on, and he teaches an evening course on testing. In his spare time he restores old radios and keeps a small garden of herbs on his balcony, which he says has taught him more about patience than any project.
