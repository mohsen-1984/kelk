# Service agreement

## 1. Parties

This agreement is made between the Client, a regional library network, and the Supplier, a company that builds and maintains search software for public archives. Each party has read the terms below and agrees to them.

The Client is represented by its director of digital services. The Supplier is represented by its managing director, who signs for the company and its subcontractors.

## 2. Definitions

- **Day** means a calendar day, including weekends and public holidays.
- **Invoice** means an official invoice approved by the tax authority.
- **Release** means a tested version of the software, delivered with its notes.
- **Service window** means the hours in which the Supplier answers support requests.

## 3. Scope of work

The Supplier installs the search software on the Client's servers, imports the existing catalogue, and trains two members of staff in its use. The work is complete when the Client confirms in writing that the catalogue can be searched by title, author, subject and date.

The Supplier also adapts the public search page to the Client's visual identity. Changes to the page after acceptance are new work and are priced separately.

## 4. Timetable

| Phase | Work | Weeks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Installation and catalogue import | 3 |
| 2 | Search page and visual identity | 2 |
| 3 | Training and acceptance | 1 |

A phase starts when the previous one has been accepted. A delay caused by the Client moves the dates of the following phases by the same number of days.

## 5. Price and payment

The price is fixed for the scope above. The Client pays thirty percent when the agreement is signed, forty percent when phase two is accepted, and the rest when the whole work is accepted. Each payment is due within thirty days of a correct invoice.

## 6. Support

For twelve months after acceptance, the Supplier fixes faults in the software at no charge. A fault that stops searching is answered within four hours in the service window; other faults within two working days.

The Supplier keeps a record of every request, its answer and the time taken, and sends the record to the Client every month.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's documents and data confidential, during the agreement and for three years after it ends. Records of the library's readers are never copied outside the Client's servers.

## 8. Ending the agreement

Either party may end the agreement with sixty days' written notice. Work accepted before the end is paid in full; work in progress is paid in proportion to what has been delivered.

## 9. Disputes

The parties first try to settle a dispute between themselves. If they cannot agree within thirty days, the dispute goes to mediation, and then to the courts of the Client's region.
