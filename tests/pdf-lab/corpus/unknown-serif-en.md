# The street orchestra

Every Friday evening a small orchestra plays in the square by the old market. It began with three friends and a borrowed drum, and now it has more than thirty players, from children who have just learned their first notes to grandparents who played in bands when they were young.

The players meet on Wednesdays in the back room of the bakery to practise. Anyone may join: the orchestra lends instruments to those who have none, and two music teachers give their time to help the beginners.

In the summer the orchestra plans to visit the villages along the river. The money for the bus comes from a jar at the edge of the square, where listeners leave what they can after each concert.
