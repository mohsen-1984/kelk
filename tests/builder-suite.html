Kelk — library tests

Every case of tests/test-corpus.js and the three about/guide documents through the four builders (.docx, .doc, .pdf, .html), in this browser. Each cell: size and time, a download link, or the error. The direction column checks that the preview (BidiView) and the builders (BidiCore) decide the same, and — for the table-dir-* cases — that every table cell takes the stated direction (a cell without letters follows its table). The preview column checks that the preview (PreviewBuilder) and the .html export (HtmlBuilder) give every block the same direction and every table cell the same lines, fill, weight and text color (setTableStyle; the table-style-* cases cover every lines and fill option and the total row). The PDF → MD column reads each PDF back with PdfImporter (layout only, no embedded source) and compares its words with the document's text, formulas left out: recall (source words found, in any order) and order (longest common run of words, as a share of the source); green ≥ 97% / 95%, grey ≥ 85%. A case with a pdfCheck also has its PDF's geometry checked in the .pdf column (pages read with pdf.js): the table-rowspan-* cases, that each cell's text starts at its column's edge (rowspan / colspan); the table-tall-* cases, that a row taller than a page is split across pages with no text off a page.
